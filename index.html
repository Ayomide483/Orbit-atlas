How it works

In live mode, your browser downloads the latest public orbit data from CelesTrak and works out every satellite position itself, using the standard SGP4 method that satellite trackers rely on. Nothing is sent to our servers, because there are none.

If live data is unavailable, or you just want a fast demo, the idealized model draws each constellation from its published altitude and inclination. It looks right at a glance, but individual positions are illustrative.

Passes and visible counts use a minimum elevation of 10 degrees above your horizon, which is a practical limit for buildings and trees.

Questions

Is the data live?

Yes, when you choose Live data in the tracker. It uses orbit data from CelesTrak, which is refreshed throughout the day, and your browser keeps a copy for two hours.

How accurate are the positions?

Usually within a few kilometers when the orbit data is fresh. Accuracy drops the further you move the clock from now, so treat far-future passes as estimates.

Do you track me or store my location?

No. There are no accounts and no analytics in this site. The location you pick stays in your browser and is never asked for through your device.

Why do the numbers differ from other trackers?

Different sites use different data sources, update times and minimum elevations. Small differences are normal.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free to use.
